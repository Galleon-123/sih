1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & MISSION PHILOSOPHY
====================================================================================================
UniServ is India's pioneer on-demand home and institutional service delivery platform built on the 
statutory framework of the Multi-State Cooperative Societies (MSCS) Act. Unlike predatory aggregator 
platforms that extract 25% to 35% commission margins from unorganized gig workers while offering 
customers no transparent pricing or enforceable service warranties, UniServ establishes an equitable, 
high-trust bridge between urban consumers and democratically governed worker cooperatives.

KEY DIFFERENTIATORS:
1. Anti-Exploitation Fair Wage Model (80/10/6/4):
   - 80% of every rupee paid goes directly to the artisan's bank account.
   - 10% is deposited into the Cooperative Worker Healthcare, Accident & Pension Welfare Fund.
   - 6% funds the District Tool Bank, Training Academy, and Local Cooperative Operations.
   - 4% covers digital platform maintenance and the Seva Suraksha statutory warranty guarantee pool.

2. Dual Operational Scales:
   - Solo Household Services: Immediate or scheduled dispatch of certified individual artisans 
     (or 2-3 artisan squads for heavy cleaning and landscaping) across 10 specialized trades.
   - Bulk & Community Projects: End-to-end turnkey project execution for Residential Welfare 
     Associations (RWAs), gated townships, educational universities, and corporate campuses 
     with milestone-based escrow financing, digital gate passes, and police-verified worker rosters.

3. Zero Trust & Physical Handshake Security:
   - Physical Start OTP handshake prevents unauthorized worker impersonation or ghost arrivals.
   - Physical Completion OTP sign-off guarantees work verification prior to payment disbursement.
   - Part Procurement Pause & Resume OTP (Phase I) for Electricians and Technicians ensures 
     workers are not penalized and customers are not billed while parts are being procured.
   - Automatic 2-Hour Overtime Surcharge (Phase II) guarantees fair wages (+₹100) for extended labor.
   - Cancellation Surcharge Protection (Phase III) prevents frivolous booking cancellations (+₹25 fee 
     transferred to artisan compensation fund on next booking).
   - 30 KYC-Verified Trade-Isolated Workers Pool (Phase IV) with zero cross-trade contamination.

4. Universal Accessibility:
   - Complete localization across 23 Scheduled Indian Languages (English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, etc.).
   - Interactive Voice Response System (IVRS) simulator for digital illiterates and senior citizens.
   - "Register for Another" assistive booking proxy flow for elderly parents and remote relatives.

4. THE 10 COOPERATIVE HOUSEHOLD SERVICES (EXHAUSTIVE BREAKDOWN)
====================================================================================================
Every household trade in UniServ is operated by a certified trade guild affiliated with a district 
cooperative society. There are strictly 10 core household trades (Home Tutor was removed as per specs):

1. PLUMBER (`s1`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹99
   - Common Diagnoses: Water pipe bursts, concealed pipeline seepage, sanitary fixture replacement, 
     overhead water tank ball-valve repair, RO water purifier plumbing, drain blockages.
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Primary plumbing issue (Tap/Faucet, Pipe Leakage, Toilet/Sanitary, Water Tank, Clogged Drain).
     * Q2: Pipe material setup (CPVC/UPVC, Galvanized Iron, Concealed Wall Piping, External Fitting).
     * Q3: Required parts supply (Customer has parts, Artisan should bring standard fittings).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Pressure joint wrenches (10" & 12"), Teflon thread seal tape, basin 
     wrench, pipe clamps, spare rubber gaskets, rotary drain snake.
   - Procurement Pause (Phase I): NOT eligible.

2. ELECTRICIAN (`s2`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹99
   - Common Diagnoses: Main MCB tripping, short circuit faults, modular switchboard replacement, 
     ceiling fan capacitor/coil replacement, decorative light installation, 3-phase wiring inspection.
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Electrical fault type (Tripping MCB, Burnt Switchboard, Fan Repair, Inverter Wiring, Earthing).
     * Q2: Electrical phase load (Single Phase 230V, Three Phase 415V, Commercial High Amp).
     * Q3: Material readiness (Artisan should bring Havells/Anchor MCB, Customer supplies wire).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Digital multimeter (CAT III 600V), 10kV insulated VDE safety pliers, 
     wire strippers, C-Curve MCB spares, continuity neon tester, heat-shrink insulation tape.
   - Procurement Pause (Phase I): ELIGIBLE (Stopwatch pauses with Resume OTP while ordering parts).

3. CLEANER (`s3`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹199
   - Common Diagnoses: Deep kitchen degreasing, bathroom calcium/limescale scrub, sofa upholstery 
     shampooing, full home post-renovation cleaning, balcony pressure wash.
   - Multi-Worker Squad Selector: Customer can select 1, 2, or 3 Cleaning Artisans:
     * 1 Worker: Standard pace (Base ₹199).
     * 2 Workers: 2x Faster pace (+₹199 extra, Total ₹398).
     * 3 Workers: 3x Rapid power clean (+₹398 extra, Total ₹597).
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Cleaning focus (Kitchen Deep Clean, Full Bathroom Scrub, Sofa/Carpet, Entire Apartment).
     * Q2: Home floor configuration (1 BHK / Studio, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, Duplex Villa).
     * Q3: Chemical preference (Eco-friendly organic enzymes, Heavy-duty stain descaler).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: High-pressure rotary scrub buffer, color-coded microfiber pads, pet-safe 
     organic cleaners, rubber squeegees, wet/dry extraction nozzles.
   - Procurement Pause (Phase I): NOT eligible.

4. CARPENTER (`s4`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹149
   - Common Diagnoses: Main door lock and latch repair, hydraulic hinge replacement for modular 
     kitchen cabinets, sliding wardrobe track realignment, bespoke furniture assembly, bed frame repair.
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Woodwork requirement (Door Lock/Latch, Cabinet Hinges, Furniture Assembly, Wood Trimming).
     * Q2: Wood/Material grade (Plywood/MDF, Solid Teak/Sheesham, Aluminium Frame, Particle Board).
     * Q3: Hardware items (Artisan supplies hydraulic channels, Customer has Godrej lock).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Precision carpentry tenon saw, soft-close hydraulic hinge channels, 
     wood chisels, adhesive fillers, digital spirit level, magnetic drill bits.
   - Procurement Pause (Phase I): NOT eligible.

5. PAINTER (`s5`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹299
   - Common Diagnoses: Wall moisture and dampness waterproofing, interior fresh coat painting, ceiling 
     seepage repair, exterior weather-proof coating, single accent wall stencil/texture design.
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Painting scope (Single Accent Wall, Full Room Touch-Up, Damp Seepage Treatment, Full Home).
     * Q2: Finish grade (Royal Luxury Silk, Premium Acrylic Emulsion, Anti-Damp Primer, Distemper).
     * Q3: Masking & protection (Cooperative drop sheets included to cover all furniture).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Low-VOC 9-inch paint rollers, anti-damp putty scrapers, sandpaper 
     sheets (80-220 grit), plastic floor masking sheets, masking tape rolls.
   - Procurement Pause (Phase I): NOT eligible.

6. CAREGIVER (`s6`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹249
   - Common Diagnoses: Post-operative patient care, bedridden elderly assistance, blood pressure and 
     vitals monitoring, medication management, mobility and wheelchair transfer support.
   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Care profile (Elderly Geriatric Care, Post-Surgery Recovery, Bedridden Patient, General Nurse).
     * Q2: Daily duration shift (Short 2-hr Assistance, 4-hr Half Day, 8-hr Full Day).
     * Q3: Special medical equipment (Oxygen concentrator present, Wheelchair mobility required).
   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Digital BP & pulse monitor, patient transfer mobility belt, pulse

7. TECHNICIAN (`s7`)
   - Starting Base Rate: ₹149
   - Common Diagnoses: Split/Inverter AC refrigerant gas charging, cooling coil repair, AC indoor unit

   - Diagnostic Questions:
     * Q1: Appliance category (Split/Window AC, Refrigerator, Front/Top Washing Machine, Microwave).

   - On-Site Certified Toolkit: Commercial PSV badge, zero-alcohol breathalyzer clearance, live GPS

5. COMMUNITY & BULK BOOKING PLATFORM (RWAs & INSTITUTIONAL CAMPUSES)
====================================================================================================
Accessible directly via the bottom navigation tab "Community" (`Screen18_CommunityScreen.js`) as well 
as the "Bulk" toggle inside the booking form (`Screen05_BookingForm.js`), this subsystem caters to 
large-scale residential societies and educational/corporate campuses.

DUAL COMMUNITY CATEGORIES:
1. Societies & RWAs (Residential Housing Societies):
   - Scope: Overhead water sump cleaning, society-wide corridor LED rewiring, plumbing audits across 
     piping shafts, compound wall painting, student hostel deep cleans.
2. Colleges & Campuses (Universities & Educational Campuses):
   - Scope: Statutory audit compliance (AICTE/UGC/NAAC standards), campus security gate pass issuance, 
     hostel mess kitchen deep cleans, academic complex sanitation.

THE 4-TIER SCALE ENGINE (Configured in `bulkServiceConfigs.js`):
- Scale 1: Micro / Wing Level (e.g. 5–15 Flats or 10,000–25,000 sq ft Department Wing)
  * Crew: 2–3 Artisans | Duration: 1–2 Days | Advance Escrow: 20%
- Scale 2: Medium / Block Level (e.g. 16–50 Flats or 50–150 Room Hostel Block)
  * Crew: 4–6 Artisans | Duration: 2–4 Days | Advance Escrow: 25%
- Scale 3: Large / Society Level (e.g. 51–150 Flats or 3–5 Floor Academic Complex)
  * Crew: 7–12 Artisans | Duration: 5–8 Days | Advance Escrow: 30%
- Scale 4: Mega / Township Level (e.g. 150+ Flats or 50+ Acre University Campus)
  * Crew: 12–20 Artisans | Duration: 8–15 Days | Advance Escrow: 35%

SPECIALIZED MATERIAL TIERS (Tailored per trade):
- Plumbing: Standard ISI UPVC Fittings vs Premium Astral CPVC & Heavy-Duty Valves.
- Electrical: Standard ISI Copper Wire vs Polycab FRLS Flame-Retardant Wire & Schneider MCBs.
- Painting: Asian Paints Tractor Emulsion vs Apex WeatherProof & DampProof Exterior Sealant.
- Cleaning: Eco-Enzymatic Biodegradable Chemicals vs Industrial Diversey Chemical Systems.

TRANSPARENT 80/10/6/4 METRIC CALCULATION:
The UI computes and visualizes real-time metrics before booking:
- Approximate Cost Range (Min to Max).
- Estimated Midpoint Cost.
- Direct Artisan Team Wages (80%).
- Worker Healthcare & Accident Fund (10%).
- District Co-op Reserve & Tool Depot (6%).
- Platform & Seva Suraksha Pool (4%).

MILESTONE ADVANCE ESCROW FINANCING:
- Bulk projects are backed by a State Bank of India (SBI) Multi-State Cooperative Escrow account.
- Customers deposit Milestone 1 Advance (20%–35%) to trigger contractor dispatch.
- Milestone 2 is disbursed upon midpoint physical inspection sign-off.
- Milestone 3 is released after statutory audit sign-off and 30-day warranty activation.

STATUTORY CAMPUS GATE PASS & PROPOSAL GENERATION:
- Gate Pass Generator: Generates digital security gate pass featuring police-verified artisan names, 
  Aadhaar KYC hashes, tool inventory list, and cooperative vehicle plate numbers for gate security.
- PDF Proposal Download: Generates official cooperative society cost estimate with government seal.

6. COMPLETE END-TO-END USER JOURNEYS & INTERACTION FLOWS
====================================================================================================

FLOW 1: SOLO HOUSEHOLD IMMEDIATE SERVICE BOOKING ("Book Now")
1. User opens UniServ -> Selects preferred regional language on `Screen01_LanguageSelection`.
2. Enters mobile number on `Screen02_OTPLogin` -> Automatically receives and verifies 4-digit OTP.
3. On `Screen03_Home`, views available categories, active cooperative banner, and nearest co-op depot.
4. Taps desired category (e.g., Electrician `s2`) -> Navigates to `Screen04_ServiceDetail`.
5. Reviews inclusions, standardized rates, and safety protocols -> Taps "Book Now".
6. `Screen05_BookingForm`:
   - Auto-detects exact doorstep GPS location with street name, locality, and pincode.
   - Answers 3 specialized trade triage questions (e.g., Tripping MCB, Single Phase, Artisan brings parts).
   - Optional: Records live audio voice note or attaches photo/video evidence of fault.
   - Selects "Book Immediate".
   - Dynamically checks price breakdown (Base rate + optional add-ons - first time user discount ₹50).
   - Taps "Find Verified Worker • ₹...".
7. `Screen06_WorkerMatch`:
   - Radar dispatch animation searches local cooperative radius.
   - Matches nearest KYC-verified artisan (e.g., Vikram Singh, Electrician, 1.2 km away).
   - Shows matching rationale (5-star rating, high completion rate, district guild member).
8. `Screen07_LiveTracking`:
   - Displays live interactive map with animated moving vehicle icon and real-time ETA countdown.
   - Shows artisan trust card, photo, vehicle plate number, masked calling button, and real-time chat.
   - Highlights 4-digit Safety PIN.
   - Displays itemized certified on-site toolkit (multimeter, insulated pliers, MCBs, etc.).
   - Displays attached voice note player and photo thumbnails.
   - (Simulated 10-minute traffic delay triggers after 4 seconds with options to Wait or Switch Artisan).
9. Artisan physically reaches doorstep:
   - User taps "Arrived at Doorstep" -> Transitions to `Screen08_StartOTP`.
   - Customer verifies artisan photo and ID badge, then shares the 4-digit Start OTP.
10. `Screen09_JobInProgress`:
    - Live stopwatch starts ticking (MM:SS).
    - Status changes to "Work in Progress — Covered under Seva Suraksha".
    - [Special Phase I]: If Electrician or Technician needs to order a part, artisan taps "Part Procurement". 
      Stopwatch stops immediately. System generates a secure 4-digit Resume OTP. When artisan returns 
      with the part, customer verifies part and enters Resume OTP to resume the stopwatch.
    - [Special Phase II]: If total active stopwatch exceeds 120 minutes (2 hours), system automatically 
      adds an overtime charge of +₹100 to the bill.
    - Worker can request extra scope approval -> Navigates to `Screen10_ExtraWorkApproval` where customer 
      inspects added items and accepts/rejects.
11. Work Completed:
    - Worker signals job finished -> Navigates to `Screen11_CompletionOTP`.
    - Customer inspects quality of work, approves, and shares 4-digit Completion OTP.
12. `Screen12_PaymentScreen`:
    - Shows transparent bill summary: Base rate + Extra work + Overtime (+₹100 if >2 hrs) + 
      Previous cancellation fee (+₹25 if applicable) - First time discount (₹50).
    - Displays 80/10/6/4 fair wage allocation bar.
    - User selects payment method: Dynamic UPI QR Code, Credit/Debit Card, NetBanking, or Cash to Artisan.
13. `Screen13_PaymentSuccess`:
    - Displays digital receipt with transaction ID.
    - Generates and allows download of Seva Suraksha 30-Day Cooperative Warranty Certificate.

7. THE 6-STAGE SERVICE LIFECYCLE STATE MACHINE & DUAL-OTP HANDSHAKE
====================================================================================================
Every booking in UniServ transitions through a deterministic, auditable finite state machine (FSM) 
managed in `BookingContext.js`:

Stage 1: `requested`
- Customer submits booking request.
- Broadcasted to nearby verified cooperative workers in the hyper-local ward.

Stage 2: `assigned`
- Primary artisan accepts the job.
- Worker photo, vehicle plate, rating, and cooperative credentials displayed to customer.

Stage 3: `on_the_way`
- Worker starts transit towards customer's location.
- Live GPS tracking, ETA countdown, and itemized certified toolkit are active.

Stage 4: `arrived` (Physical Handshake #1 - Start OTP)
- Worker arrives at doorstep.
- Customer verifies physical identity against app photo and ID badge.
- Customer shares the 4-digit Start OTP (`start_otp`).
- Worker inputs OTP to verify presence and unlock the active job timer.

Stage 5: `work_started`
- Active labor underway. Stopwatch timer runs continuously.
- Covered under Seva Suraksha insurance pool.
- Supports Phase I Part Procurement Pause and extra work approvals.

Stage 6: `completed` (Physical Handshake #2 - Completion OTP)
- Worker finishes service and tests all fixtures.
- Customer inspects results.
- Customer shares 4-digit Completion OTP (`completion_otp`) to authorize final billing.
- Navigates to transparent payment settlement.

8. SPECIAL ALGORITHMIC PHASES (PHASES I, II, III, IV)
====================================================================================================

PHASE I: PART PROCUREMENT PAUSE & SECURE RESUME OTP
- Problem Solved: In solo home services, if an artisan starts work and discovers that a specific spare part 
  (e.g., specific MCB, capacitor, compressor relay) is required that takes hours or days to procure, 
  traditional apps let the timer run, resulting in unfair charges to the customer.
- Rule: STRICTLY for Electrician (`s2`) and Technician (`s7`). Other trades are not eligible.
- Mechanism:
  1. Artisan requests part procurement pause -> Customer accepts on screen.
  2. Stopwatch timer immediately freezes at current elapsed seconds (`timerAccumulatedSeconds`).
  3. System generates a cryptographically secure 4-digit Resume OTP (`procurement_resume_otp`) and displays 
     it exclusively to the customer.
  4. Timer remains completely stopped with zero tick accumulation even across app reloads.
  5. When the worker returns with the purchased part, customer enters the Resume OTP into the app.
  6. Timer safely resumes from the exact frozen second.

PHASE II: AUTOMATIC 2-HOUR OVERTIME SURCHARGE (+₹100)
- Problem Solved: Cooperative workers must be fairly compensated when complex diagnostic tasks take longer 
  than the standard 2-hour window.
- Rule: Common to ALL 10 cooperative household services.
- Mechanism:
  1. The stopwatch monitors total active labor time (`activeSeconds`).
  2. If `activeSeconds > 7200` (120 minutes / 2 hours), the system automatically triggers the overtime flag.
  3. An automatic overtime surcharge of +₹100 is appended to the bill calculation (`overtime_surcharge: 100`).
  4. Clear warning and explanation banner is rendered in the payment breakdown so the customer knows 
     why the fee was applied.

PHASE III: CANCELLATION SURCHARGE PROTECTION (+₹25)
- Problem Solved: Frivolous customer cancellations after an artisan has already accepted and traveled 
  waste artisan fuel, time, and lost wage opportunities.
- Mechanism:
  1. On the live tracking screen (`Screen07_LiveTracking`), a "Cancel Service" button is provided.
  2. If the customer taps Cancel while the artisan is assigned/en route, an alert warns:
     "If you cancel now, our cooperative artisan has already reserved time and fuel for your visit. 
     A nominal cooperative cancellation fee of ₹25 will be charged on your NEXT service booking."
  3. If confirmed, the booking is terminated and `pendingCancellationFee: 25` is persisted in user state.
  4. On the very next booking created by this user, the ₹25 fee is automatically attached (`cancellationPenalty: 25`).
  5. The booking form and final payment screen render a red penalty banner showing the +₹25 fee.
  6. Upon successful payment of the next booking, the pending cancellation penalty is reset to ₹0.
